Explain Gauss's law and when to use it
Gauss's Law states that the total electric flux through a closed surface equals the enclosed charge divided by ε₀.

When to use it:
• Spherical symmetry (point charge, charged sphere)
• Cylindrical symmetry (infinite wire, charged cylinder)
• Planar symmetry (infinite sheet of charge)

The key insight: pick a Gaussian surface where E is constant and perpendicular to the surface.
Give me a practice problem
Here's one:

A charge of +5 μC is placed at the centre of a cube. What is the flux through each face?

Hint: Use Gauss's law and symmetry.
